## Selection metadata
- Slug: `cupertino`
- Tagline: Photo-forward personal keynote — full-bleed imagery, monochrome SF Pro, a countdown of numbered lessons, one blue "one more thing."
- Mood: personal, premium, reflective, cinematic
- Register: speaker-led · Density: low · Scheme: dark · Formality: medium-high
- Best for: "N things I learned from a chapter of my life/career" story talks — when you have real photos and a numbered list of takeaways. Reflections, career retrospectives, farewell/anniversary keynotes, founder or maker origin stories.
- Avoid for: abstract/imageless talks (use Obsidian), hype launches (Noir/Spotlight), data-dense reading decks.

## Visual snapshot
The premium monochrome base of Obsidian — true-black stage (#000), near-white SF Pro (#F5F5F7),
weight 600 — but the black is **filled with your own full-bleed photography** under a directional
scrim, not left empty. The deck is a literal countdown: a personal story arc, then a **giant
counting numeral** over a hero photo, then lessons `1…N` that each reveal one at a time (the
number punches in blurred, the headline clip-wipes in beneath it), then a **recap** of all of
them, then a **QR close** to your personal site. Everything stays monochrome except a single
**Apple-blue** word spent once, on the "one more thing." Reads as an expensive, deeply personal
Apple-style keynote.

## Preview ingredients
- Palette: stage #000 (faint depth radial on text-only slides); ink #F5F5F7; muted #8E8E93; directional photo scrim rgba(0,0,0,.35→transparent); ONE accent, Apple blue #2997FF, used a single time.
- Typography: SF Pro Display 600; tabular-nums for the count and lesson numbers; type over photos carries a soft dark shadow (never a glow).
- Signature move: a giant counting numeral (~58cqmin) over a bright, Ken-Burns hero photo.
- Signature move: per-lesson reveal — a big chapter number blurs/punches in, the headline clip-wipes in beneath it, one lesson per full-bleed photo.
- Signature move: a recap slide listing all N lessons as a numbered column beside one anchoring image; a QR close that hands the room your world.
- Motion: calm blur-in fade-rise (~1s); Ken Burns on photos; the counted number-landings are the choreography. No bounce, no confetti.
