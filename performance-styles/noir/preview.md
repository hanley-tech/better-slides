## Visual snapshot
A black stage (#000–#0B0C12) with near-white type (#F5F5F7) and a single restrained accent.
Type is enormous and tightly tracked — one statement per slide, acres of negative space. The
deck reads like a darkened auditorium: the screen is mostly black so the few lit words carry
all the weight. The signature is theatrical *time* — slides hold a beat before the line
lands — and the closing "one more thing."

## Preview ingredients
- Palette: stage black #0B0C12; ink #F5F5F7; muted #8A8A8E; one accent (default electric blue #2997FF), used only on the kicker, one hero word, and rules.
- Typography: display in a tight grotesque (Inter Tight / Helvetica Neue 900) at massive scale; mono kicker.
- Signature move: one idea per slide in huge type, centered or hard-left, with a single accent word.
- Signature move: held-pause pacing — the headline arrives on a beat, not on slide entry.
- Signature move: the `omt` finale — a near-empty hold, then "one more thing", then the payoff line slamming in.
- Sound (if enabled): swoosh on section breaks, chime on the hero number, applause bed under the finale.
