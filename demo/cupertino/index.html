A talk about making things

Nobody asked for any of it.

Five things ten years of unpaid nights taught me
2015 — a borrowed garage

I spent ten years making things nobody asked for.

Most of them died quietly. The few that didn’t rearranged my life.
5

Five of them stayed with me.

1

Nobody is watching. That’s the gift.

An empty room is permission to be bad at something in public.
2

Finish something ugly.

One finished ugly thing teaches more than ten perfect plans.
3

The work compounds. You don’t.

Guard the sleep. The project will still be there in the morning.
4

Say it before it’s ready.

The version you’re embarrassed by is the one that finds people.
5

The road shows up when you drive.

Nobody hands you the next chapter. You make something, and it appears.
